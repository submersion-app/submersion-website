The complete dive app, free and open.

For recreational and technical divers, Submersion downloads 358 dive computer models, analyses each profile with Bühlmann ZH-L16C, plans open-circuit and rebreather dives and tracks equipment service. It is free and open source under GPL-3.0. Your data is stored on your devices and synced only through storage you choose.

Submersion downloads, logs and analyses every dive, and keeps sites, trips, photos and equipment together.

The whole dive

Logging and analysis

Submersion covers a dive from the computer download to long-term statistics. Every field is optional.

Download and import

Dive computer download

Submersion downloads over Bluetooth LE and USB. After the first download only new dives transfer, and known computers connect automatically.

358 supported models

Support comes from libdivecomputer and covers 38 manufacturers. iOS supports Bluetooth only. See the tested models by platform.

Duplicate review

A download that matches an existing dive is shown beside it. You can skip it, import it as a new dive, replace the earlier source or merge both computers into one dive.

Imports

Submersion imports files from Subsurface, MacDive, Shearwater Cloud, Diving Log, DAN DL7, UDDF, Garmin FIT and CSV. Suunto, Garmin Connect and divelogs.de import by signing in.

Paper logs and Apple Health

Scanned logbook pages are read with OCR for review. On iOS, Apple Watch underwater workouts import with depth, temperature and heart rate.

The log

Trips and itineraries

Each trip has an itinerary and a page per day, with the day's dives on a map, a timeline and the photos.

Home

The Home page shows totals, recent trips, overdue service and the latest profile.

Conditions

Weather is added for the date and location. Tides are calculated offline from a global model.

People

Each dive records buddies and their roles, with signatures.

Search

Search accepts plain words, for example "Bonaire last year".

Sites and species

Sites and species identification

Species are identified on the device in photos, and tracked through video with labels.

20,999 sites

The bundled catalogue includes 20,999 sites and 3,593 dive centers from OpenStreetMap and other open sources. Map regions can be saved for offline use.

3D seafloor

Site terrain is shown in 3D with depth contours and steep walls, where bathymetry is available.

Photos on the profile

Photos and video are matched to the dive by capture time and placed on the profile at their depth. Underwater colour correction is built in.

Platforms

Photo identification runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, Windows and Linux, and video identification on all except Linux. It needs a one-time model download, and 8 GB of memory on Android.

Analysis

Profile analysis

Any point on the profile shows the deco status, oxygen exposure and loading of all 16 tissue compartments, calculated from the profile.

Bühlmann ZH-L16C

The model uses gradient factors and tracks nitrogen and helium. A heat map shows all 16 compartments over the dive.

Cylinders and gas

Each cylinder lists its mix, start and end pressure, gas used, SAC, RMV and MOD.

Multiple computers per dive

Downloads from every computer worn on a dive attach to that dive and can be overlaid on one chart.

Playback and 3D

Playback supports about twenty overlays, from ppO2 to gas density. The profile can also be viewed in 3D.

Insights

Statistics and connections

Twelve pages of statistics cover depth and time trends, air consumption and conditions. Connections maps how sites, trips, equipment, species and buddies relate.

Progression

Maximum depth, runtime and bottom time are charted per dive with a rolling average.

Connections

There are nine built-in maps, such as trips and people or sites by species. Any site, buddy or item of equipment can be placed at the centre.

Observations

Observations note changes such as dives getting deeper or longer than the previous year.

Records

Personal records such as the deepest and longest dives have their own page.

A safer diver

Safety

Submersion reviews dives, calculates plans and tracks equipment. It is not a dive computer and does not replace training, a buddy or your computer.

After the dive

Post-dive safety review

Each dive is checked for fast ascents, missed deco and safety stops, sawtooth profiles, a high surfacing gradient factor, late gas switches and isobaric counterdiffusion. Findings show the time and the value.

Severity

Findings are graded info, caution or significant. Each rule can be turned off.

Buoyancy

Net buoyancy is modelled across the dive, with a suggested lead weight for the next one. What if recalculates for different lead, water or exposure suit.

Oxygen exposure

CNS and OTU are totalled per dive, per day and over seven days.

Data source

NDL, deco stops, TTS and CNS can each come from the dive computer or from Submersion's calculation.

Before the dive

Dive planning

Plans for OC, CCR, SCR and pSCR are built segment by segment. The deco schedule, gas consumption and contingencies update with each change.

Bailout

On loop plans, bailout gas is checked against the worst point of the dive, which is marked on the chart.

Contingencies

Deeper, longer and lost-gas variants are calculated, along with a range table.

Repetitive dives

Dives planned as a series carry tissues, CNS, OTU and cylinder pressures forward.

Gas calculators

Calculators cover MOD, best mix, MND, consumption, rock bottom and gas density. The blender uses real-gas fills.

Equipment

Equipment and service

Each item has service intervals by date, dives, hours and exposure to salt, cold and oxygen. Equipment sets are shown on a diver figure with numbered items.

Regulator and O2 service

Regulator service is due at 365 days or 100 dives, or after 50 cold dives. O2-clean intervals count hours on high oxygen.

Condition checks

A short post-dive check-in feeds ten rules that flag equipment trends.

Passports and tags

Cylinders and equipment can carry QR or NFC tags. Without the app, a scan opens the cylinder's passport or the item's details on this site.

Transmitters and reports

Transmitters are linked to their cylinders. Service and inventory reports export as PDF.

Ready to dive

Certifications and checklists

Each certification shows its last use and when a refresher is due. Pre-dive checklists run from templates, and the emergency card works offline.

Currency

Built-in rules cover rebreather, decompression and other certifications. Custom rules can be added.

Checklists

Templates include a BWRAF check, GUE EDGE, a CCR build with O2 cell linearity checks and gear packing.

Emergency card

The card holds emergency numbers, nearby chambers, contacts and medical notes, and works offline.

No-fly time

A no-fly countdown runs from the last dive using the selected published guideline.

Your data stays yours

Your data

Submersion is free and open source under GPL-3.0. It has no account, ads or subscription.

Storage

Storage and sync

Data is stored on your devices. Sync runs through Google Drive, Dropbox, S3, WebDAV or Git, or iCloud on iPhone and iPad, with optional end-to-end encryption.

Nearby devices

Paired devices sync over Bluetooth without a cloud service.

Encryption

The database can be encrypted at rest, and the app locked with a password, Face ID, Touch ID or Windows Hello.

Backups

A backup is made before every database upgrade. Scheduled backups can go to a folder or cloud storage, optionally encrypted.

Photos

Photos and videos are linked from your library or folders, not copied.

Export

Export and data quality

Dives export as UDDF 3.2, CSV, Excel or PDF logbooks, and sites as KML. The data quality check flags records that disagree with sensor data and offers a repair.

Raw data

Raw dive computer data is kept, so dives can be re-parsed when the parser improves.

Change log

Every edit is logged with its previous value and can be reverted by field or by operation.

Agent access

On desktop, an AI assistant can access the logbook through a local server with a token. Proposed changes are previewed before they are written.
