Buddy card

A diver shared their buddy card with you

A buddy card holds a diver's name, contact details and certifications, so you can add them as a buddy in Submersion.

Get Submersion, then scan the code or tap the tag again. The card opens in the app, where you choose what to keep.

This page does not show the card. Its details stay in the link on your phone and are never sent to this site.
