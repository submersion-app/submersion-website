Emergency tags

An emergency tag is an NFC tag or a printed card that a diver carries on their gear.

It holds their allergies, emergency contacts and dive insurance. Whoever helps the diver taps the tag with their own phone and reads the details on this page.

The details are in the link itself. This page reads them in your browser and sends nothing to a server.

You write your own emergency tag from your emergency card in Submersion.
