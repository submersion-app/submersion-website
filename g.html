Gear tags

A gear tag is a QR label or NFC tag on a piece of dive gear other than a cylinder: a regulator, a BCD, a computer, a light.

You scan it with your phone to open that item's gear passport in Submersion: its service status, recent service and use. Without the app, this page shows what the tag says.
